Privacy policy

Applies to the Wicktail game on Google Play, the desktop app and the web version at mickrodrigues.github.io/wicktail. Last updated 10 November 2026.

In short: Wicktail does not collect, sell or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases. Your progress is saved only on your own device.

What the game stores, and where

To remember your progress, Wicktail saves a small amount of game data in your device's local storage:

  • which levels you have unlocked, your best times, crate gems, dream shards and orb counts
  • the last checkpoint you reached in each level
  • your settings, such as volume, graphics options and control bindings

This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. You can delete it at any time with Settings → Gameplay → Reset all progress, by clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or by uninstalling the game.

Internet access

The Android app contains all of its game files and does not need an internet connection to play. The web version loads its game files, fonts and code libraries from GitHub Pages, jsDelivr, cdnjs and Google Fonts. Like any website, those services receive your IP address and browser details when your browser downloads files from them, under their own privacy policies. Wicktail itself does not log or receive this information.

Feedback emails

If you tap Send feedback or Email, the game opens your own email app with a draft addressed to wicktail.support@gmail.com. The draft includes the game version, level, device type, screen size and graphics setting so we can look into problems. You can read and edit everything before sending, and nothing is sent unless you send it yourself. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use it only to reply and to improve the game.

Children

Wicktail does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect personal information at all.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy or the game: wicktail.support@gmail.com
