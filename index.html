The Game

A PS2-style platformer, built for the dream.

Flint Wicktail is the last lantern left in a dream the Moon wants to swallow. Run, jump and spin through haunted villages, slime markets, letter-eating bogs and a mill with teeth. Smash every crate, find the hidden shards, and take down the villains standing between you and the sky.

9 hand-built levelsFrom Sleepy-Sun Village to the Moon's own sky, each with its own tricks, chases and secrets.
8 boss fightsA sleepy sun, a cauldron goblin, a mimic, a mill, a raven, a king, a dragon gate and the Moon's Teeth himself.
Voice-acted storyThe Moon's Teeth commands his servants in fully voiced cutscenes.
Play your wayKeyboard and mouse, PlayStation or Xbox controller, or touch. Every button can be rebound.
Smooth on any screenUp to 240 FPS with graphics presets, resolution scale and field-of-view settings.
Crash-style challengeThree flames, limited lives and checkpoints to fight your way back from.
