The Game

A PS2-style platformer, built for the dream.

Flint Wicktail is the last lantern left in a dream the Moon wants to swallow. Run, jump and spin through haunted villages, slime markets, letter-eating bogs and a mill with teeth. Smash every crate, find the hidden shards, and take down the villains standing between you and the sky. Then, in Chapter 2: The Stolen Stars, ride the Moonline, wake a drowned library, cross a burning bridge and storm Vesper Quill's floating citadel to put the stars back where they belong.

14 hand-built levelsFrom Sleepy-Sun Village to Vesper's Star-Cage Citadel, each with its own tricks, chases, secrets and story.
13 boss fightsNine hits each, three phases, no cheap damage. From a sleepy sun to the Moon's Teeth, then the Bellwarden, the Root Regent, Abbess Undertow, Baroness Cinderwing and Vesper Quill.
Voice-acted storyVoiced cutscenes, talking allies and diaries to find, plus puzzles, set pieces and rides between the fights.
Play your wayKeyboard and mouse, PlayStation or Xbox controller, or touch. Every button can be rebound.
Smooth on any screenUp to 240 FPS with graphics presets, resolution scale and field-of-view settings.
Crash-style challengeThree flames, limited lives and checkpoints to fight your way back from.
