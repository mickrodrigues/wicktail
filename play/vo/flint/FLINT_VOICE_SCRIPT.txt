How to record:

  - Say each line 2-3 times with slightly different energy; send the best.
  - Save each as .mp3 (or .m4a/.wav, I'll convert) with the EXACT file name.
  - Keep a little gap of silence before and after each line.
  - Flint: brave even when nervous, cheeky to bullies, kind to scared folk.
    Sound like a small hero, not a cartoon mascot. Natural > "voice acting".

Any line you don't record is simply skipped in the game, nothing breaks.
